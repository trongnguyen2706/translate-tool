Downloads and sync

Offline dictionaries

Removing a dictionary keeps your saved words and sync queue. You can download it again from the extension popup.

Saved words

Only entries for the signed-in account are shown. Retry keeps the original operation ID to prevent duplicate flashcards. Removing a local entry does not delete words already synced to your library. Uninstalling the extension deletes unsynced entries.

AI request history

Completed responses are reused. An unknown outcome is never retried automatically. Allowing another attempt can use another AI request.
